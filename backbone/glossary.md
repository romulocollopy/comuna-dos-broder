# Backbone — Glossary

Defined terms. The contract will capitalize these and define them once. PT (Brazilian) equivalents given where the legal term differs.

| Term (EN) | PT | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| **Member / Participant** | Membro / Participante | A natural person who signs the agreement and makes contributions. |
| **Organizer** | Organizador | The person(s) who set up and administer the initiative; may also be a member. |
| **Vehicle** | Veículo | The legal entity that owns the land and holds the fund (company/SPE, partnership, co-op, or trust — per branch). |
| **Contribution / Aporte** | Aporte | Any money or money-equivalent (services, goods) a member puts in. |
| **Contribution statement** | Termo de aporte | The dated, signed record of a contribution: type, value, unit of account, reserve allocation. |
| **Land reserve** | Reserva de terra | The principal fund used to buy land; backs ownership stake. |
| **Insurance reserve** | Reserva de seguro / garantia | The self-insurance pool (a % of contributions) used to pay withdrawals; reverts to principal on success. (Contractual reserve fund, *not* an insurance product.) |
| **Stake / Quota** | Quota / Participação | A member's proportional interest, derived from land-reserve contributions. |
| **Unit of account** | Unidade de conta | The currency in which obligations are measured (BRL, CAD, or dual-currency rule). |
| **Indexation** | Correção monetária | Adjusting obligations by inflation (IPCA / CPI). |
| **Withdrawal / Exit** | Retirada / Saída | A member voluntarily leaving and reclaiming value. |
| **Vesting schedule** | Cronograma de aquisição de direitos | The graduated schedule determining how much a withdrawing member receives based on when they exit. |
| **Default** | Inadimplência | Failure to meet a contribution obligation. |
| **Forfeiture / Dilution** | Perdimento / Diluição | The consequence of persistent default: reduction or loss of stake. |
| **Success milestone** | Marco de sucesso | The defined event (e.g. land purchased and registered) after which the unused insurance reserve reverts. |
| **Right of first refusal** | Direito de preferência | Members' right to buy a departing member's stake before any outsider. |
| **Shotgun / buy-sell clause** | Cláusula de compra e venda forçada | Anti-deadlock mechanism: one party names a price; the other must buy or sell at it. |
| **Super majority** | Maioria qualificada | A vote threshold above simple majority (e.g. 2/3), for reserved matters. |
| **Lex rei sitae** | Lei do local do bem | The principle that land is governed by the law of where it is located. |
| **Self-insurance** | Auto-seguro | The group bearing its own withdrawal-liquidity risk via a reserve, rather than an insurer. |
| **Reversion** | Reversão | The flow of unused insurance reserve back to the land reserve on success. |
