# Backbone — Proposal Template

A fill-in-the-blanks pitch to present to a group of people once the uncertainties are addressed. Keep it human — this is not the contract, it's the invitation. Replace every `{...}`.

> Suggested working title: **"{Name} — buying land together, on purpose."**

---

## 1. The idea, in one breath

We are {N} friends. Over {T} years we each put in what we can — money, or real work and materials. Together we buy land. Eventually we build homes and live there, together.

## 2. Why this way

- **Nobody has to be rich to join.** Uneven contributions are expected and fair.
- **Life happens.** If someone needs out, they get their money back on terms everyone agreed to *up front* — no awkward renegotiation.
- **We protect each other.** A small slice of every contribution goes into a shared reserve, so an exit never forces the group to sell land at a bad time.
- **If all goes well, that reserve comes back to us.**

## 3. How the money works

- You contribute **{monthly amount / lump sum}**, or equivalent in **{services/goods}**.
- **{100 − x}%** goes to the **land fund** (your ownership stake).
- **{x}%** goes to the **safety reserve** (the group's self-insurance).
- Your **stake** = what you've put into the land fund. Unequal contributions mean unequal stakes — that's by design.

## 4. What you get

- **Ownership** proportional to your stake, held through a proper legal entity (not a handshake).
- **A vote.** You help decide when and what we buy, who joins, and the big calls.
- **A place**, eventually, to live — for the long term.
- **A way out.** You can leave, and here's exactly what you'd get back: {summary of vesting schedule}.

## 5. What happens if…

| If… | Then… |
|---|---|
| You need to leave early | You get your contributions back (less the safety slice), per the agreed schedule |
| You can't keep paying | Grace period, then your stake adjusts — but it doesn't hurt the others |
| We hit our goal | The unused safety reserve comes back to the group |
| We disagree on a big decision | We vote; and there's a fair tie-breaker in the contract |
| The land loses value | We share the loss proportionally, same as we'd share a gain |

## 6. How decisions get made

Day-to-day runs itself. The big calls — buying land, selling, adding someone, changing the rules — need a **supermajority**. Money decisions follow your stake; communal decisions are one-person-one-vote.

## 7. The legal backbone

This is backed by real law, not goodwill. The structure is {vehicle}, governed by {jurisdiction}, with a written contract that a lawyer has reviewed. We're transparent about the risks (see the risk summary). {Country-specific note: e.g. "Because the land is in Brazil, Brazilian law governs the property."}

## 8. How to join

1. **Read** the full proposal and the risk summary. Take your time.
2. **Ask** anything.
3. **Sign** the agreement and make your first contribution.
4. **Stay in the loop** with {statements/meetings cadence}.
